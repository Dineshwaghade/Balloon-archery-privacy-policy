Privacy Policy

Bowburst: Balloon Archery · Last updated 25 September 2026

The short version. The game keeps your progress on your own phone and sends none of it anywhere. There is no account and no sign-in.

The one thing that does leave your device is advertising data, collected by Google AdMob so it can show the ads that keep the game free. You can limit or reset that at any time, and everything below explains how.

Who this is about

This policy covers the Android game Bowburst: Balloon Archery (package com.waghadestudio.balloon_archery), published by Waghade Studio ("we", "us").

Questions about anything here: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved in the app's own private storage on your phone. It never leaves the device, we cannot read it, and it is not backed up to us:

  • High score, best wave, best chain, runs played and balloons popped
  • Coins, bow upgrades, which bows you have unlocked and which one is equipped
  • Daily missions, your daily streak and the day you last played
  • Settings: music volume, sound volume and whether haptics are on
  • Whether you have finished the tutorial, and whether you have been asked to rate the game

Deleting it is entirely in your hands: Settings → Reset progress inside the game clears it, and uninstalling the app removes it completely.

What third parties collect

Google AdMob — advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including ads you choose to watch in exchange for arrows or coins. To do that, Google may collect and process:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier)To select ads, cap how often you see the same one, and measure whether an ad worked
Device information: model, operating system version, language, screen size, network typeTo deliver an ad the device can actually display
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressTo show ads relevant to your country or region, and to apply the right privacy laws
Ad interactions: ads shown, watched to the end, tappedFraud prevention, reporting and payment

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. What it does with it is set out in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google Play services

The game asks Google Play to show its in-app rating card once, after your second run. Google handles the rating itself; we never see who rated or what they gave. Google Play also handles downloading and updating the app, under Google's Privacy Policy.

We collect nothing ourselves

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter that phones home to us, no server of our own, and no account system. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and the app requests no permission that would let it reach any of them.

Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to fetch ads. The game itself plays entirely offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — so AdMob can read the advertising ID described above.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's own consent form, the first time you open the game. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy inside the game.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and turn off ad personalisation there. The game keeps working; the ads simply become less relevant.
  • Your saved progress can be wiped from inside the game, or by uninstalling.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask Google for a copy of the data it holds about you, or to have it deleted. Because that data sits with Google and not with us, those requests go to Google through the links above.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, write to us at Swaghadestudio@gmail.com and we will act on it.

How long data is kept

Your on-device save lasts until you reset it or uninstall the game. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention schedules, described in the Google links above.
